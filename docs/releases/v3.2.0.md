# ChitChat v3.2.0

`v3.2.0` is a maintenance release on top of `v3.1.0`. It adds optional guest access, so visitors can look around without an account in rooms that allow it, and creating rooms from Administration. It applies one forward-only database migration, `0039_guest_access.sql`, so upgrading needs a backup first.

## Project status: feature-complete

This release does not change [project status](../README.md#project-status-feature-complete). ChitChat remains feature-complete. The changes below are specific, maintainer-requested additions of the kind the maintenance-only policy reserves room for. See [`docs/roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md).

## Added

- **Guest access, off by default.** ([#133](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/133), [#134](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/134))
  - A Super-Administrator switches it on under **Operational settings → Access**. The sign-in page then offers **Look around as a guest**. Switching it off ends every guest visit at once, and a maintenance lockdown pauses new ones.
  - Each visit is a numbered guest such as "Guest 0042", and numbers are never reused. A visit lasts one browser session: it ends after two idle hours, after 24 hours at most, or when the guest leaves. An ended guest keeps its name on past messages.
  - Rooms choose under **Guests** whether guests may not enter, may read, or may read and write. Only public rooms without a minimum age qualify. A guest sees only those rooms and joins one by opening it.
  - Guests cannot send or receive direct messages, ping, mention or be mentioned, upload attachments, search, or hold a role. Their `@names` notify nobody, and they post more slowly.
  - Guests wear a dashed **Guest** badge and outline wherever their name appears, and have their own group in the members panel.
  - A banner and a welcome notification point guests to registration, and rooms they may only read say so in place of the message box. Appearance choices made as a guest last only for the visit.
  - On a guest's profile card, Global Moderators and up can **End guest session** or **Block guests from this connection** for an hour, a day or a week. The moderator never sees the address.
  - Starting a guest session solves the registration proof-of-work puzzle, is rate-limited per address, and allows at most three guests per connection at a time.
- **Create room in Administration.** Super-Administrators, Administrators and Chat Admins can create a room from the Rooms tab, with the same settings as in the chat, and land on its settings, members and invitations. The tab now shows for them even before the first room exists. ([#135](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/135))

## Fixed

- The Search button under "Invite a user" in Room administration ran past the edge of its card on mid-sized screens. ([#136](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/136))

## What to evaluate

Operators staging this release should exercise:

- switching guest access on and off, and the **Guests** choice on a few rooms (not allowed, read, read and write; disabled for private rooms and rooms with a minimum age);
- a guest visit end to end: starting it, which rooms appear, writing where allowed, the read-only notice, the banner and welcome notification, "Create an account", and "Leave";
- as a member, how guests appear on messages, in the members panel and on the profile card, and that direct messages, pings and mentions never reach a guest;
- as a Global Moderator, ending a guest's visit and blocking guests from a connection;
- creating a room from Administration;
- existing login, MFA, passkey, provider sign-in, room, direct-message, attachment, search, moderation, export, retention, maintenance and reverse-proxy SSE behaviour remaining unaffected.

## Database changes

`0039_guest_access.sql` makes these changes:

- **`users`:** adds `account_kind` (`member` or `guest`) and guest-only expiry, last-seen and address columns, with a constraint tying them together.
- **New objects:** adds the `guest_number_seq` sequence and the `guest_blocks` table.
- **Settings and rooms:** adds `system_settings.guest_access_enabled` (default off), and `rooms.guest_access` (default `none`, allowed only on public rooms without a minimum age).
- **Notifications:** adds the `guest_welcome` notification kind.

Existing accounts become `member` accounts. The migration is forward-only: older source must not be pointed at the migrated database.

## Upgrade from v3.1.0

1. Stop or drain application writes.
2. Create and verify a complete PostgreSQL-plus-attachment backup set.
3. Deploy `v3.2.0` source.
4. Compare `.env` with `.env.example`. Two optional rate-limit variables were added, `RATE_LIMIT_GUEST_START_*` and `RATE_LIMIT_GUEST_ROOM_SEND_*`; the defaults apply when they are absent.
5. Run:

   ```sh
   composer install --no-dev --classmap-authoritative
   composer migrate
   composer maintenance:dry-run
   ```

6. Verify `/health.php`, `/ready.php`, login, sending a message, and creating a room from Administration.

Guest access stays off until a Super-Administrator switches it on. The daily maintenance job closes guests that idled out and drops expired connection blocks. The result reports these as `guests_ended` and `expired_guest_blocks`.

Upgrading from before `v3.1.0` also applies that release's migrations; see its notes.

## Known limitations

Unchanged from `v3.1.0` (see its [known limitations](v3.1.0.md#known-limitations)), plus the following:

- A guest cannot turn their visit into an account. Creating an account ends the visit, and the guest's earlier messages stay under the guest name.
- Guests cannot search messages, even in the rooms they may read.
- Connection blocks work by IP address, so guests behind one shared address (an office, a mobile carrier) are blocked together. Members on that address are not affected.
