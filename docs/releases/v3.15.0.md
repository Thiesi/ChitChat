# ChitChat v3.15.0

`v3.15.0` gives the web client **Lantern Next**, a visual overhaul of every page: calmer controls, quiet lists with one lit row, a conversation that keeps the words in front, toasts at the bottom, and a few small moments of motion that stay still for anyone who asks for reduced motion. It also fixes a keyboard focus slip after sending, a room being redrawn twice while the page settles, an unneeded session request on every resize, and two accessibility details. It has **no database migrations, no configuration changes and no API changes**, and nobody is signed out.

## Project status: feature-complete

This release does not change [project status](../README.md#project-status-feature-complete). It contains maintainer-approved presentation and accessibility work, tracked in [#381](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/381) (Lantern Next) and [#163](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/163). See [`docs/roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md).

## Lantern Next

Lantern Next keeps Lantern's colours, type and mark and removes the drift between screens. It shipped in eleven slices, L0 to L10. The reasoning is in [`docs/design/lantern-next-direction.md`](../design/lantern-next-direction.md), and every brand-level change is in [`docs/design/lantern-next.md`](../design/lantern-next.md).

- **Foundations.** ([#383](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/383))
  - Light themes get a slightly deeper paper, so white surfaces stand out from it without outlines.
  - Lantern's dark theme gets a deeper colour for your own messages, which makes their text easier to read.
  - Conversations sit on their own lighter "stage", which separates the message bubbles more clearly. Midnight keeps true black.
- **One set of controls.** ([#385](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/385))
  - Buttons are pills everywhere, and the main button is flat.
  - Fields show one focus ring instead of two.
  - Unread counts and the "@" mark have one size.
  - A person is drawn the same way everywhere: their colour with their initials.
  - A room's badge shows the first letter of its name instead of "#". Screen readers still hear the room as before.
- **A quiet frame.** ([#390](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/390))
  - The navigation rail shows only the installation's icon, without lines.
  - The installation's name heads every list (Rooms, Direct, You) at every width and is never cut short.
  - Activity, Search and the stewardship pages share one page header.
- **Lists with one lit row.** ([#391](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/391))
  - The open room or conversation is marked once: a lit row, a short bar at its start, and its name in bold.
  - Section labels are in sentence case instead of capitals.
  - The mention strip's "Open" is an outlined pill instead of an underlined link.
- **The conversation.** ([#392](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/392))
  - Bubbles hold words: a run of messages shows its time once. Every message keeps its exact time, shown on the bubble under the pointer, with keyboard focus, and to screen readers as before.
  - A message's actions take no room until the pointer or focus is on it, so the name line stays on one line on phones.
  - The message box starts slimmer, and its hint is shorter.
  - A message that arrives while you're reading rises gently into place.
  - An empty room shows a larger, lit drawing.
- **Signing in.** ([#395](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/395))
  - The drawing beside the form is a small conversation that settles into place once.
  - Sign in / Register is a segmented switch.
  - The second sign-in step has one main button.
  - "on ChitChat" and "Powered by ChitChat" carry the one-colour Lantern mark. The mark family is described in [`docs/design/mark.md`](../design/mark.md) ([#227](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/227)).
- **Direct, Activity and Search.** ([#396](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/396))
  - In Direct, Report and Block open from one "More actions" menu at every width. It's a full menu button: arrow keys move between the items, and Escape returns to the button.
  - Activity notices are rows. One with somewhere to go can be clicked anywhere, and "Mark as read" is a round tick at its end.
  - Search is one quiet bar.
- **You.** ([#398](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/398))
  - Section titles are smaller, and settings cards recede.
  - Colour schemes are chips. The chosen one has a ring, a bold name and a tick, so it isn't shown by colour alone.
- **The stewardship pages.** ([#400](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/400))
  - Administration, Operational settings, System status, the Moderation queue and the inspection pages share one quiet card.
  - Members and invitations are rows.
  - The open case in the queue is marked by a bar as well as by colour.
  - The Dusk stewarding frame and the warnings for sensitive access stay as they were.
- **Toasts and dialogs.** ([#403](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/403))
  - Toasts sit at the bottom, centred, above the message box or the phone's bottom bar, and never cover them. They take no clicks.
  - An error toast shows a warning sign as well as its colour. What screen readers hear is unchanged.
  - Dialogs settle into place, and a phone's sheet rises from the bottom over a dimming backdrop.
  - With reduced motion nothing moves, now on every page, the stewardship and status pages included.
- **The other empty places and the status pages.** ([#406](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/406))
  - An empty Direct conversation, an Activity with nothing new ("You're all caught up.") and a search that found nothing show the empty room's drawing.
  - The "not found" and "unavailable" pages get the same care.
  - Where the browser supports it, going from page to page is a short cross-fade with the rail holding still. It's skipped with reduced motion.
- **The handoff.** ([#407](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/407)) [`docs/design/lantern-next.md`](../design/lantern-next.md) is the finished design document that ChitChat Mobile's own redesign starts from.

## Fixed

- **A late send no longer pulls keyboard focus back to the message box.** ([#322](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/322), [#408](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/408)) If sending took a moment and you had already moved on, for example to the emoji picker, focus used to jump back to the message box when the send finished. Now it returns there only if it was still there, or had been lost. The same applies to sending and uploading in Rooms and in Direct.
- **Choosing a room again doesn't redraw it under you.** ([#402](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/402), [#405](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/405)) While the page settled after sign-in, the same room could be chosen more than once. Each choice redrew the conversation when its load arrived, which lost your place and moved messages from under the pointer. Now the latest choice draws the room once.
- **Resizing the window no longer asks the server for the session.** ([#367](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/367), [#409](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/409)) The sign-in page's Register tab is kept in step with the server's setting. It re-asked on every resize across a phone, tablet or desktop width, and it now asks only when you sign in or out.
- **Unread counts keep their border in forced colours.** ([#378](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/378), [#380](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/380)) In Windows contrast themes, the ordinary unread counts in Rooms and Direct showed as bare digits. They now have a border, like the quiet counts and "@". The current navigation tab and the unread badges are now tested in forced colours, and the [accessibility statement](../accessibility.md) claims them again.
- **The accessibility link's hint wraps as one piece on phones.** ([#377](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/377), [#379](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/379)) "(opens GitHub)" no longer splits across two lines. An operator's long address still breaks inside if it can't fit on a line of its own.

## What to evaluate

Operators staging this release should check that:
- Rooms, Direct, Activity, Search and You show the new look in light and dark, and the installation's name heads each list;
- a toast (for example after uploading a file) appears at the bottom without covering the message box;
- in Direct, "More actions" in the conversation's header offers Report and Block;
- with reduced motion turned on in the system, nothing moves.

## Upgrade from v3.14.0

1. Stop or drain application traffic (see `INSTALL.md`, Upgrading), and back up PostgreSQL and attachment storage together.
2. Deploy `v3.15.0` source.
3. Run:
   ```sh
   composer install --no-dev --classmap-authoritative
   composer migrate
   ```
   `composer migrate` has nothing to apply.
4. Reload PHP-FPM, and restart the push dispatcher listener if you run one.
5. Verify `/health.php`, `/ready.php`, and that `GET /api/v1/session.php` reports `application.version` `3.15.0`.

Upgrading from before `v3.14.0` also applies that release's migration (`0061`); read its release notes first.

## Fresh installation

Follow `INSTALL.md`, or `docs/operations/netbsd-apache.md` on NetBSD with Apache.

Publication validation checks those named results on the exact 40-character merge commit before the annotated `v3.15.0` tag and stable GitHub release are created.

## Known limitations

- **A message's "More" by tap on a real phone is unverified** ([#394](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/issues/394)). Browser emulation can't settle whether the actions on a message's name line open by tap. On touch screens, a tap on the bubble opens a sheet with the same actions.
- Otherwise unchanged from `v3.14.0`. The [accessibility statement](../accessibility.md) lists what is and isn't tested.
