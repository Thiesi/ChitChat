# ChitChat v3.1.0

`v3.1.0` is a maintenance release on top of `v3.0.0`. It lets moderators and administrators act where they see a problem, on the message or on the person, and adds muting. It applies one forward-only database migration, `0038_user_mutes.sql`, so upgrading needs a backup first.

## Project status: feature-complete

This release does not change [project status](../README.md#project-status-feature-complete). ChitChat remains feature-complete. The changes below are specific, maintainer-requested additions of the kind the maintenance-only policy reserves room for. See [`docs/roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md).

## Added

- **Delete on the message.** Everyone allowed to moderate a room sees **Delete** next to Report on other people's messages, with a confirmation and an optional reason for the audit log. Before, a moderator had to report a message to themselves to act on it. ([#127](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/127))
- **A Moderation part on the profile card** behind every name, showing only what the viewer may do. ([#129](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/129))
  - In the open room: make or remove a room moderator, mute, remove from the room.
  - Everywhere: sign out, mute, ban, lift a ban or mute, and open the person in Administration.
- **Muting.** ([#129](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/129))
  - A muted person can still sign in and read, but cannot post, edit, react, ping, upload or show as typing. Muted everywhere also stops sending direct messages.
  - Room owners and moderators mute in their room; Global Moderators and up mute everywhere.
  - The person is told why and until when, in a notification and in place of the message box.
- **Lengths and room notices.** ([#129](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/129))
  - Bans and mutes take a preset or a custom length (minutes, hours or days, with the end time shown) and an optional reason.
  - Muting in a room, banning and removing from a room can **let the room know** with a neutral line such as "Alex was muted in this room for 1 hour.", without the reason or the moderator's name.
- **Actions in the moderation queue.** A case offers **Delete message**, **Mute author** and **Ban author** where the moderator may act. Each also closes the case with the matching outcome and note. ([#130](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/130))

## Changed

- **Moderation follows ranks.** ([#127](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/127))
  - The order is member < room moderator < room owner < Chat Admin and Global Moderator < Administrator < Super-Administrator.
  - Moderators act only on lower ranks; only Super-Administrators act on their equals.
  - So a room moderator can no longer delete the room owner's or global staff's messages, and only a Super-Administrator may sign out, ban, reset the password of, or change the roles of an Administrator.
- **No password confirmation for signing out, banning and lifting a ban.** They are reversible and audited. Changing roles and resetting passwords still ask. ([#127](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/127))
- **Google and Twitch logos.** The provider buttons show their logos, drawn in the page, so nothing is fetched from either company. ([#128](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/128))

## Fixed

- The Report message dialog's buttons looked different from each other in rooms and direct messages. ([#126](https://github.com/Thiesi/ChitChat/pull/126))

## What to evaluate

Operators staging this release should exercise:

- as a room owner, room moderator, Global Moderator, Administrator and Super-Administrator, which actions appear on messages and on the profile card, and that they work;
- muting in a room and everywhere: what the muted person sees in rooms and direct messages, the notification, lifting the mute, and a mute running out;
- banning with a custom length, and "let the room know" for mutes, bans and removals;
- deleting, muting and banning from a case in the moderation queue;
- existing login, MFA, passkey, provider sign-in, room, direct-message, attachment, search, export, retention, maintenance and reverse-proxy SSE behaviour remaining unaffected.

## Database changes

`0038_user_mutes.sql` adds the `user_mutes` table and the `muted` notification kind. It is forward-only: older source must not be pointed at the migrated database.

## Upgrade from v3.0.0

1. Stop or drain application writes.
2. Create and verify a complete PostgreSQL-plus-attachment backup set.
3. Deploy `v3.1.0` source.
4. Compare `.env` with `.env.example`; no new variables were introduced.
5. Run:

   ```sh
   composer install --no-dev --classmap-authoritative
   composer migrate
   composer maintenance:dry-run
   ```

6. Verify `/health.php`, `/ready.php`, login, sending a message, and the profile card behind a name.

Upgrading from before `v3.0.0` also applies that release's migrations; see its notes.

- authenticated unbuffered SSE delivery through real Nginx and PHP-FPM.

Publication validation checks those named results on the exact 40-character merge commit before the annotated `v3.1.0` tag and stable GitHub release are created.

## Known limitations

Unchanged from `v3.0.0` (see its [known limitations](v3.0.0.md#known-limitations)), plus the following:

- Moderators cannot delete other people's direct messages. Reports and the inspection tool cover private conversations.
